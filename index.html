OxyLinkPro（以下简称「本应用」）是一款用于扫描、连接附近蓝牙（BLE）制氧相关设备，并在手机上查看设备状态与运行数据的工具类应用。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用会如何处理信息。

运营主体：Jetlee

1. 我们处理哪些信息

1.1 不会收集的信息

本应用不会收集、存储或上传以下信息：

  • 姓名、手机号、邮箱、账号、密码等个人身份信息
  • 精确或大致位置
  • 通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风
  • 健康档案、医疗记录、血氧等人体生理数据（本应用展示的是设备输出的运行参数，不是您身体的健康指标）
  • 广告标识符（IDFA）或其他用于广告追踪的信息
  • 支付信息、浏览记录、分析/崩溃统计数据

本应用没有用户注册、登录、云同步、推送、统计分析或广告 SDK。

1.2 仅在本机处理的信息

为提供扫描、连接和查看数据的功能，本应用会在您的设备本地、通过系统蓝牙接口处理以下信息。这些信息不会离开您的手机（不会上传至我们或任何第三方）：

类型内容用途
蓝牙权限与附近设备信息 附近 BLE 设备的名称、信号强度（RSSI）、系统分配的外设标识、广播中的序列号（如有） 扫描并列出可连接设备，供您选择连接
设备标识信息 设备序列号（SN）、固件版本、连接地址类信息 完成握手后，在设备页展示当前设备
授权码 您根据仪器提示输入的验证码 仅用于与设备完成本地鉴权/握手，不保存
设备运行数据 氧浓度、流量、机器状态、定时时间、单次运行时间、累计运行时间 仅用于在界面实时展示

上述信息均驻留在应用运行时的内存中，不会写入相册、文件、钥匙串，也不会使用 UserDefaults 等持久化存储进行保存。断开连接或退出应用后，上述信息即被清除。

本应用声明使用了蓝牙后台模式（bluetooth-central），以便在系统允许的范围内维持与已连接设备的通信。我们不会在后台收集额外信息，也不会进行定位。

2. 我们如何使用信息

仅用于：

  1. 扫描附近 BLE 设备并展示列表；
  2. 连接您选择的设备，完成验证码鉴权与协议握手；
  3. 在本机界面展示设备版本、序列号及实时运行数据；
  4. 处理连接失败、超时、断开等状态提示。

我们不会将上述信息用于广告、画像、营销，也不会出售或共享给第三方。

3. 信息共享、转让与公开披露

我们不会与任何第三方共享、转让或公开披露您的信息。本应用不包含第三方统计、推送、广告、社交或支付 SDK。

系统蓝牙由 Apple 的 Core Bluetooth 框架提供。设备发现与连接遵循 iOS 系统权限与隐私控制；您可在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 蓝牙」中随时关闭对本应用的授权。

4. 数据存储与跨境传输

  • 存储地点：仅在您的 iPhone 本地内存中临时处理，我们不运营用于存储用户数据的服务器。
  • 存储期限：不进行持久化保存；断开设备或退出应用后即不再保留。
  • 跨境传输：因不上传数据，不涉及将个人信息传输至境外。

5. 您的权利

您可以：

  • 在系统设置中关闭蓝牙权限（关闭后将无法扫描和连接设备，核心功能不可用）；
  • 在应用内点击「断开连接」，停止与当前设备的通信；
  • 卸载本应用。因我们不保存您的数据，卸载后无需另行申请删除云端数据。

如对本政策有疑问，请通过文末联系方式与我们联系。

6. 未成年人保护

本应用面向成年人使用制氧相关设备时的辅助查看，不面向 13 周岁以下（在中国大陆为 14 周岁以下）的儿童。我们不主动收集未成年人的个人信息。如您是监护人并认为未成年人向本应用提供了信息，请与我们联系。

7. 健康与医疗说明

本应用用于查看所连接设备的运行状态参数（例如设备输出的氧浓度、流量、开关机状态和运行时长），不是医疗器械，不提供诊断、治疗或监护建议。展示数据来自所连接设备，仅供参考，不能替代专业医护人员的意见。身体不适请及时就医。

8. 政策更新

我们可能适时修订本政策。更新后会在本页面公布新的生效日期。若变更导致处理方式发生实质性变化，我们将通过应用内提示或更新说明告知。继续使用本应用即表示您了解更新后的政策。

OxyLinkPro (“the App”) lets you scan and connect to nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) oxygen-related devices and view device status and live operating data on your iPhone. This Policy describes how information is handled.

Operator: [Legal Entity Name]

1. Information We Process

1.1 Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or upload:

  • Name, phone number, email, account credentials, or other identity information
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Health records or physiological measurements from your body (the App shows device operating parameters, not your personal health metrics)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or data used for tracking
  • Payment information, browsing history, analytics, or crash reports

The App has no registration, sign-in, cloud sync, push notifications, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

1.2 Information Processed Only on Your Device

To scan, connect, and display data, the App processes the following locally via the system Bluetooth APIs. This information does not leave your iPhone:

TypeExamplesPurpose
Nearby Bluetooth devices Device name, RSSI, system peripheral identifier, serial number from advertisement (if present) List devices so you can choose one to connect
Device identifiers Serial number (SN), firmware version, connection address-type information Show the current device after handshake
Auth code The verification code you enter as shown on the instrument One-time local authentication with the device; not saved
Device operating data Oxygen concentration, flow rate, machine status, timer, session time, accumulated time Live on-screen display only

This information is held in memory only. It is not written to Photos, files, Keychain, or UserDefaults. It is cleared when you disconnect or quit the App.

The App may use Bluetooth in the background (bluetooth-central) so communication with an already-connected device can continue as allowed by iOS. We do not collect extra information in the background and we do not use location services.

2. How We Use Information

Solely to:

  1. Scan for nearby BLE devices and show the list;
  2. Connect to the device you select and complete auth-code handshake;
  3. Display version, serial number, and live operating data on your phone;
  4. Show connection errors, timeouts, and disconnect status.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and we do not sell or share it.

3. Sharing

We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your information. The App includes no third-party analytics, ads, social, or payment SDKs.

Bluetooth is provided by Apple’s Core Bluetooth framework. You can revoke Bluetooth access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

4. Storage and International Transfer

  • Where: Processed only in memory on your iPhone. We do not operate servers that store user data.
  • How long: Not persisted; discarded on disconnect or when the App is closed.
  • Cross-border transfer: Not applicable, because no data is uploaded.

5. Your Choices

You may:

  • Turn off Bluetooth permission in iOS Settings (scanning and connecting will stop working);
  • Tap Disconnect in the App to end the current session;
  • Uninstall the App. Because we do not store your data, there is no cloud copy to delete.

Contact us using the details below if you have questions.

6. Children

The App is intended for adults using related equipment. It is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in mainland China). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Guardians who believe a child has provided information may contact us.

7. Health Disclaimer

The App displays device operating parameters (such as output oxygen concentration, flow, on/off status, and run time). It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or monitoring advice. Values come from the connected hardware and are for reference only. They do not replace professional medical advice. Seek medical care if you feel unwell.

We may update this Policy and will post the new effective date on this page. If changes are material, we will provide notice in the App or release notes.
